## 7 November 2026 – Snow: a windy mountain pass above the clouds (prototype)

**Snow**, Frozen Peak's own look, used to be a snowy forest at dusk, close to Moonlit Snow. Now it has its own place: a high mountain pass in hard daylight, above the clouds. On the 3D board:
- **A mountain pass:** the battlefield lies in the saddle of the pass. Steep mountain shoulders rise on both sides behind the cliffs, rock below and snowfields above. Past the enemy's end the pass drops away over a lip of snow into a sea of clouds, with crags and far-off peaks rising out of it.
- **Bright high-altitude light:** a pale sky and a low white sun. Snow in the sun is white, and everything in shade turns cobalt blue.
- **A glacier across no man's land:** smooth turquoise ice with frozen bubbles and polished shine. Deep under the ice, right in the middle, lies the faint shadow of a mammoth frozen long ago. The deploy zones are wind-packed snow, carved into sharp ridges that all point the way the wind blows.
- **The wind:** loose snow streams sideways across the board in gusts, snow blows off the ridges in plumes, and tattered flags snap on their poles at the top of the pass. The wind gets stronger every round, and in a long match a white haze closes in around the edges.
- **Rock and ice, not forest:** faceted charcoal cliffs and boulders with snow caps and icicles line the sides, with teal ice blocks and crystal clusters between them. Only a few dead trees are left.
- **The yeti:** when your opponent presses Ready while you're still deploying, a yeti pops up from behind a big rock, grins and waves at you, then ducks back down. If the fight starts while he's up, he ducks at once.
- **Diamond dust:** tiny ice crystals glitter in the sunlit air, and the ice catches the sun in flashes.

The rules don't change, and Frozen Peak's hail and altar stay switched off. Moonlit Snow looks the same as before.

## 7 November 2026 – Forest Glade: Darkwoods gets its own identity (prototype)

**Darkwoods**, the Standard map's own look, was Frozen Peak without the snow. It's now an ancient forest called **Forest Glade**, and the battlefield is its one clearing:
- **Giant twisted trees** lean over the sides and far corners of the board, two to three times the height of the old pines, and throw their shadows across it. Further out the woods turn into a green-black dark.
- **Eyes in the dark:** pairs of pale eyes open between the trunks, blink, close and turn up somewhere else. More of them watch every round.
- **Ground mist** drifts between the trees, and faint **light shafts** fall through the canopy onto the clearing. Glowing spores rise slowly through the air.
- **The old gate:** past the far end stands a stone gate with broken fences on either side and a shrine and candles before it, flickering warm in the dark. An old path of stones leads there from the board.
- **A new forest floor:** moss and leaf litter, dappled light, old roots creeping in from the woods on both sides, and a broken stone road across no man's land (the ice and the mountain rock are gone).
- **Undergrowth:** ferns, tall wispy grass, orange shelf fungus and mushrooms, fallen logs by the sides.
- **The lost explorer:** when your opponent presses Ready (online or the AI, while you still deploy), an explorer with a far too big backpack comes running out of the woods along the old road. He stops in the middle, looks left, looks right, squints at his map, scratches his head (a **?** pops up over him), shrugs and hurries on into the woods. If the fight starts before he's off the board, he panics (**!**) and sprints away. On the 3D board.
- **Forest Clearing:** its twisted trees now have moss-green leaves too (they were dark red).

Trees and fungus from Quaternius' Stylized Nature MegaKit; the gate, shrine and candles from KayKit's Halloween Bits; the logs from KayKit's Resource Bits (all CC0).

## 7 November 2026 – A new music player and your own playlists

- **A new look for the music player:** dark glass with low-poly facets, gold trim and corner brackets, glossy gold orbs for the Play button and the note and a thin gold progress bar.
- **Playlists roll up out of the player:** tap *Now playing* and the playlists roll up from the player, which stays where it is. Tap it again, the arrow, Escape or anywhere else to roll them back down.
- **Tabs:** *Menu Playlist* (the menu songs), *All music* (every song in the game) and your own playlists. Pick a tab's *Play* or any song in it, and the player carries on through that playlist.
- **Make your own:** the **+** tab makes a playlist from All music. Name it and tap songs to add them (they play in the order you tap). Your playlists have *Edit* and *Delete* buttons.
- **In a match:** the music button opens the same playlists, with the map's own music first as **<map> Adaptive music** (for example *Forest Glade Adaptive music*). It plays deployment music, then battle music. Pick any other playlist and it plays through deploy and battle instead. Adaptive music's *Play* goes back to the map's music.
- **Saved to your account:** your playlists and sound settings (volume, music and sound effects on or off, shuffle, repeat, the playlist playing on the menu) are kept on your account. Log in on another device and they're there too. Playlists you made before logging in are added to your account's.

## 7 November 2026 – Playtest fixes

### For every mode and map

**Units**
- **Meteor Caller:** its meteors no longer hit enemies inside its minimum range (16 m). A spider biting the caller no longer brings the meteor down on itself and the swarm. A meteor still splashes everything around where it lands.
- **Truespear:** spears thrown at the enemy it is locked on to fly past everything in between (skeletons, chaff) and follow that enemy, so they no longer miss a target walking sideways. With Dual Spear, the main spear stays on the locked enemy and the second spear takes someone else.
- **Brute, Bullhead:** charge damage 85 (was 100) and knockback 5 m (was 6). The Keep's Brute guards share it. The skill text now says it charges on the first attack, then every 5th. Its extra armor while charging starts the moment after it sets off, so two Brutes charging each other at the same time now trade evenly.
- **Rollers:** roll 9 m past their target before turning back (was 6).
- **Necromancer, Healing touch:** heals only allies within 25 m, and never towers or walls. With nobody wounded in reach, nothing is healed.
- **Necromancer, Raise dead:** raised skeletons now level up with the Necromancer (x2 health and damage at level 1, x3 at level 2) and get the Skeleton Archer skills you own.
- **Cog Builder:** a Cog Builder busy with a Factory or Altar site (walking to it or building it) no longer keeps its side in the fight: once your other units are dead, the round is lost. If its site is knocked down, the builder fights on as usual. Its 1.5 s turret build is unchanged.
- **Factory:** it only counts as built by surviving a fight when its side didn't lose that fight. After a lost fight it keeps the work done so far. The Factory sheet and the Claude opponent's rules now say so.

**Direction arrows and moves**
- **Direction arrows:** a squad walking its arrow now stops only for an enemy blocking the way ahead. Enemies beside or behind it get hit in passing. Slows and knockback no longer make it drop the arrow: walking back after being knocked back counts as getting closer. It gives up only after about 3 s without getting closer (stuck on a wall or in a crowd).
- Drag the head of a direction arrow to change where it points, no sheet needed; drop it on its squad to clear it.
- **March order (Bastion):** the free move now goes at most 2 tiles from where the squad stood at the start of the round. A longer move is the paid move (or Otto's horseshoe move). While you drag, tiles only the paid move reaches are dimmer.
- **Level all** now spreads levels: every squad that is ready gets its next level before any squad is levelled twice (lowest level first, then most XP).
- Hovering or tapping an enemy squad shows its range ring on every tile of a big unit, on the 3D board's tilted view over the model itself, and in 2v2 for your teammate's and the other opponent's squads.

**Match flow**
- **Replay a round:** when your opponent taps Next game or Rematch, or says GG, you now see it at once, next to Back to results. If the next game starts while you're replaying, you're taken straight to it.
- Replay a round is no longer offered once you've tapped Next game or Rematch.
- **GG:** when your opponent says GG you now get a banner and a sound wherever you are: on the result card, on Look at board, or in Replay a round. If you're signed in and have already gone back to the menu, it still reaches you there.
- Say GG now shows "Sending…" until the server has it, and it goes again by itself if the connection dropped.
- **Leader draft:** round 1 now tells you what winning it is worth: the winner picks a leader first in round 2. The round 1 result card says who picks first, also when time ran out (more health left) or on a draw (coin flip).
- Replays of fights from before this patch may play out a little differently.

### 2v2

**The board**
- No man's land is one row deeper (the board is 24 x 19). Its new middle row is the same distance from both teams' zones and belongs to nobody: no flank tiles and no Bastion walls there, and neither team has priority there when hidden things land on the same tile. Units that can stand in no man's land (Forward planting, Assassins, Shadowhands, Coal mine exits, Magic lake tiles) may use it.
- Both artifacts (rounds 2 and 8) now stand on that middle row, mirrored, at least 10 tiles apart and out of the two flank columns on each edge, so each is the same distance from both teams' front lines. If Coal mine exits or lake tiles fill the middle row, they stand one row nearer a team as before.
- Team A's tower and Vault moved one row back with the bigger board (tower row 17, Vault row 16).
- A phone running an older version of the game can't join a 2v2 room any more (the board changed shape). It shows "The game was updated. Please reload the page."

**Buildings**
- Your teammate's Cog Builder Factory now shows on your board while you deploy, built or being rebuilt. Tapping it opens a read-only sheet. The other opponent's shows from the round after they place it.
- A building that is out of action this round now looks it while you deploy: greyed out, nothing turning or smoking, with a small crossed-cog badge. This covers the 2v2 Factory being rebuilt, a Coal mine that is shut, and a Cog Builder's Factory being rebuilt after it was destroyed (it no longer looks like a brand-new site). Same on the 2D and 3D boards.
- **Coal mine** (new rule from Vilhelm): a mine destroyed in a fight is shut the next round, so nobody goes down it. It still stands in the fight, its exit still blocks the tiles round it, and you can still buy its skills.
- **Coal mine, Dig a new exit:** you can now buy it again, once a round per mine. It costs 150 the first time and 50 more for each earlier dig. Use it the round you buy it: one you don't use is lost when the next round begins. Taking it back refunds it. After you buy it, the tiles you can dig to light up straight away.
- With a unit still selected in the shop, tapping a building now opens its sheet (and lets go of the unit) instead of doing nothing. Move the exit also lets go of a selected unit.
- Both Factory sheets have a Read more. It explains that a unit you buy fights in that round's fight only and doesn't come back, and when it walks out (2 s or 4 s, sooner with Master engineering or the Blessing on a Cog Builder's Factory). You get no refund if the Factory falls first, and a unit that survives keeps your side in the fight but deals no Team HP damage.
- **School: new Respec.** Once a round, for free, swap an either-or skill of the unit type in your School for the other one, once that squad has sat a fight out inside. It counts in that round's fight, the enemy doesn't see it until the fight, and it stays even if the School is destroyed. Undo respec (same round) gives the use back.

## 7 November 2026 – Three new looks: Ashlands, Moonlit Snow and Savanna

Looks change only how the board and the land around it look, never the rules. Pick them in the look picker on any map that has looks (Standard, Old standard, 2v2), or get them from Random.

- **Ashlands (prototype: a battlefield):** no more forest. The land round the board is churned ash and char, pitted with shell craters, with only a few charred snags left standing. The battle's debris lies everywhere: volleys of spears stuck in the ground, shields, swords and skulls, broken palisades and stakes, charred logs, torn banners of both armies, and the great skull and bones of a fallen giant beast. A ruined fort stands past the far end with embers in its rubble, and beyond it the fire still burns, lighting the far side orange. Smoke rises from the craters, embers smoulder in them, grey ash drifts down while embers rise. No man's land is charred dark with faint glowing cracks, and old blast scorches mark the board. The dragon is never seen, only its shadow racing over the field and the land beside it each time your opponent clicks Ready (against the computer, a moment into each deploy). Debris from KayKit's Dungeon Pack, Skeletons, Halloween Bits and Resource Bits (CC0).
- **Moonlit Snow:** a snowy night. Blue moonlight, a fresh pale snow floor with no man's land as blue cracked ice (so the zones are easy to tell apart), thick wind-blown snow drifts rolling over the land round the board, snow piles where other looks have boulders (a few rocks still poke through), snow-capped pines and drifts, falling snow, and a log cabin past the enemy's end of the board: warm lit windows, light spilling onto the snow, firewood by the wall and smoke from the chimney. It's Christmas here: each treasury is a pile of wrapped presents on a mound of snow, a lantern stands at each corner of the board, casting a warm pool of light on the snow, and snowmen stand by the cabin and along the edges (presents, snowmen and lanterns from KayKit's Holiday Bits, CC0). Plays the snow music. Everything round the board fades into the night the further out it stands, the way snow looks in the dark: colours wash out and shadows turn blue rather than black. Further out the woods sink into near-total darkness under a black sky, as if the moonlight only spreads from the battlefield. The battlefield stays lit (a little softer than before, so it doesn't glare against the night), and the cabin's warm light is left out of the blue, so its windows glow.
- **Savanna (prototype of its new identity):** golden hour on the open plain. A sea of tall dry grass surrounds the board, and the wind combs it in long waves you can see rolling past, even from straight above. Wide-spaced, flat-topped acacias stand in it, with grey-olive thorn scrub and red termite mounds. Past the far end, granite outcrops of piled boulders rise from the grass, with a lone giant acacia beside the biggest. Vultures circle high overhead, and their shadows glide across the board. They always fly smoothly, even while nothing else on the board moves. In an online match, when your opponent is ready and you're still deploying, the vultures gather and circle low over your tower. If they bother you, tap one: they all fly off and sit in a tree by the board. Tap one again to bring them back. They still come to circle your tower when your opponent is ready, then go back to their tree. Between the tree and your tower they never cut across the board: they fly off screen and come back in from the edge nearest where they're going. Dust hangs in the low sun, shadows are cool violet, and no man's land is red trampled earth. The sun sinks a little every round, so a long match ends at dusk (acacias from Quaternius' Simple Nature Pack, CC0).

## 6 November 2026 – 2v2: buildings, artifacts and the draft

2v2 is still a developer test. The Assassin and Bastion changes are for every mode and map.

### For every mode and map

**Assassin**
- **Place it anywhere.** An Assassin can go on any free tile of the board from round 1: your zone, no man's land, the enemy's zone, even the flank tiles. Moves work the same way. The only rule: not on or next to a building (towers, treasuries, the Factory, the Altar, elf towers, your teammate's buildings, the 2v2 team buildings). Allowed tiles light up as usual when you pick it.
- **They won't see it coming.** An Assassin you put in the enemy's zone stays hidden from them until the fight, like every new squad. They might put a squad on that same tile without knowing. In 2v2, as the fight starts, the team whose zone that tile is keeps its squad there, and the other one moves to the nearest tile it may stand on: your Assassin in their zone moves if they put a squad on its tile, whichever came first (Vilhelm's "prio" answer below; in no man's land the one put there later moves). An Assassin that ends up next to a building it couldn't see (one the other team put down this round) stays in that spot.
- **It lands, then strikes.** In its first fight, an Assassin in the enemy's zone or on a flank tile stands still for 3 s, like a flank squad (not with a Shadow Den, below).
- **It ignores buildings.** It never goes for the tower, the treasury, walls or any other building while there is an enemy unit it can reach. If nothing else is left, it attacks buildings after all, so a fight never gets stuck. A wall right in its way it breaks through.
- **Bloodrush: 3 bleed per hit** (was 2).
- The AI sometimes drops its Assassins on your back rows now. Watch your back.

**Bastion**
- **Defensive walls are free** (were 250 gold). You still get them once a match, from the tower's sheet. The AI's Bastion takes them straight away.

### 2v2: the Vault

- **The Vault** stands where the treasury used to be, one per team. Each of you can put up to 200 gold a round into it or take up to 200 out; the first move of the round makes it a put round or a take round for the whole team. Tap the Vault to see what's inside and to move gold.
- **Compound interest** (200 gold, once a match): the Vault's gold grows 10% at the start of every round.
- **Coin golem** (100 gold each): when both of you click it in the same round, all the Vault's gold becomes a Coin Golem that fights next to the Vault this round. The more gold, the bigger it is: bronze, silver, and gold from 900.
- **If the Vault is destroyed** in a fight, 80% of its gold is lost and each opponent gets 50 gold. It stands again in the next fight.
- The loan works as before (every player's own).

### 2v2: artifacts and the draft

- **Artifacts** (rounds 2 and 8): two glowing orbs stand in no man's land, each inside a ring. Tap one to see the card it gives (a tower or a building). Each card is drawn when its artifact appears, from every tower and every team building but the Vault, and the four artifacts of a match are all different: no tower or building shows up twice.
- **Holding one:** in the fight, every squad with a unit inside the ring scores its tier in points a second for its team (a tier 3 squad scores 3, however many bodies it has; summons score 1, towers and buildings nothing). Both teams score at the same time.
- **First to 100 locks it,** and the team with more points wins it: +50 gold for each of its players. If the fight ends before that, more points wins; a tie goes to nobody.
- **Your units go for it:** units that come near an unlocked artifact walk into its ring. Melee units still go after enemies close by; ranged units stay inside and shoot only what they can reach from there. Once an artifact is locked, they fight as usual.
- **The meter:** around each orb, your team's bar (blue) climbs one side and theirs (red) the other. A locked artifact turns your colour or theirs and shows a lock. Tap the orb in the fight to see the score.
- **The artifact draft replaces the round 3 tower vote.** The cards of round 2's artifacts are dealt in round 3, round 8's in round 9. Win both and your team picks one first (the other team gets the one left); win one each and each team gets its own; an artifact nobody won goes to the team without a card. If nobody won either, a coin flip says who picks. Either of you can pick; you have 15 seconds.
- **Your tower is an Outpost until your team gets a tower card.** A tower card in round 9 replaces the tower you have (its skills, walls and mage general go with it).
- **Team buildings:** a building card opens a placement vote. Tap one of the glowing tiles; your teammate sees your vote in your colour. The same tile places it at once; two different tiles: one of the two after 15 seconds. Buildings go inside your zone (not on its outer edges: the front row and the columns next to the flanks count as edges too) and at least 2 tiles away from every other building, unless the building says otherwise. Nobody can deploy on a building's tile.
- **Building sheets:** tap a building to see what it does, its downside, and its skills. Either of you can buy them (whoever buys pays; only your own buys can be taken back). The other team can open the sheet too, but not what you bought this round.
- **The draft and the votes never hold the deploy clock.** Each has its own 15 seconds beside it. If the deploy clock runs out first, they are settled as the fight starts, just as their own 15 seconds would: the draft makes its random pick, a vote takes one of the two tiles (or the one tile voted, or the first allowed tile).
- 1v1 is unchanged.

### 2v2: the ten team buildings

**Tree kojja** (defensive): a small watchtower grown into a tree. It shoots like the Outpost, reaches 20 m and sees stealthed units in that range.
- *Grow another* (100, once a round for the team): whoever buys it places a new kojja within 3 tiles of one of your kojjas. You place it yourself, with no vote and no 2-tile room needed. Once someone has bought a skill on the new kojja, Grow can't be taken back.
- *Nest* (50, once per kojja): the kojja holds one ranged squad, yours or your teammate's: place it on the kojja or move it in. Only one body shoots from inside, with +3 m range and stealth sight. After 10 s without seeing an enemy, or when the kojja falls, the whole squad steps out and fights on foot. Nest can't be taken back while a squad is in it.
- Downside: a kojja destroyed in a fight stands for the other team the next round. It shoots for them, and they can use its Nest (or buy one) and put a ranged squad of theirs in it, even though it is on your board. The round after, it is yours again. A squad standing in a kojja when it changes hands steps out to the nearest free tile of its own zone.

**Scorch turrets** (defensive): each of you places your own turret, whenever you like (neither of you waits for the other). A turret is one Ember Imp with Spitfire (burn), instant shots and +10 m range, and it always sees stealthed units. 450 health, 2 armor. It isn't a unit, so the other team doesn't need to destroy it to win the round.
- *Blaze* (100): splash 5 m and +25% hit. *Rapid fire* (100): +25% attack speed and +5 m range. *Legs* (100): the turret walks and counts as a unit. Each turret has its own skills, and either of you can buy them.
- Downside: a turret destroyed in a fight sits out the next fight and loses all its skills.

**Field hospital** (support; 600 health, 2 armor, can't shoot): squads you or your teammate deploy on the 8 tiles round it get +20% health and regain 2% of their health a second in the fight. Every round your team wins with the hospital still standing: +5 Team HP (never above your starting 200). Downside: destroyed, −15 Team HP.
- *Field dressing* (50 gold, +50 for each of your own clicks this round, as often as you like): a random squad of your team gets +25% health for this round's fight (picked when the fight starts, so nobody can tell beforehand).
- *Second wind* (100 gold, once a round for the team): pick one of your team's squads. The first time it is wiped out in the fight it comes back whole, at full health, on the tile in front of the hospital. Either of you can move it to another squad for free once it is bought. The opponents don't see it. A squad that gives itself up on purpose (Ember Imps summoning their Hellfiend) wasn't wiped out: nothing comes back. A squad brought back keeps its Home ground, its Mark Bounty hunt and the like.

**The Oak** (support; 1200 health, 3 armor, can't shoot): *Restoration*: while the Oak stands, every heal and every bit of regeneration on your team is 25% stronger (healers, water healing, pools, healbots, lifesteal, regen...). Downside: the round after it was destroyed, a level 3 Ent (one body, all four of its skills) fights for the opponents, in front of their tower.
- *Heal* (50 gold, once a round each): +5 Team HP, counted before this round's fight takes its damage (never above 200). The results screen lists it.
- *Double restoration* (100 gold, once a round for the team): Restoration is +50% this round.
- *Sticky moss* (200 gold, once a match): every tile within 3 tiles of the Oak turns to moss for good. Enemies on it move 30% slower (the strongest slow counts; Berserkers can't be slowed). Both teams see the moss.

**Coal mine** (technical): your team votes for the entrance in your zone, then for the exit in no man's land. Squads deployed on the 8 tiles round the entrance go down the mine when the fight starts and come up 2 seconds later on the matching tile round the exit, then stand still for 1.5 seconds (as a squad new on a flank tile) before they fight (Rogues, Assassins and Shadowhands of a team with a Shadow Den don't wait). While they are underground nobody can see or hit them. Nobody (either team) can deploy on the 8 tiles round the exit, even where they are the other team's zone. **The exit can be blocked:** if, as the fight starts, the tile a squad would come up on is taken (a squad, a building, water or a wall, of either team), that squad doesn't go down the mine and fights from its own tile; if the exit's own tile is taken, nobody goes down. The exit and its 8 tiles never make anything move. The entrance can be destroyed (squads already on their way still come up); the exit can't be hit.
- *Dig a new exit* (150 gold, once a match): move the exit once, anywhere in no man's land or to a flank tile of the enemy's half. Tap the mine, then a glowing tile. You can take it back the same round only while the old exit tile is still free.
- *Fresh air* (200 gold, once a match): squads coming up the mine (and the healbots and Water Elementals that come with them) are 25% faster and have +4 armor for 5 seconds.

### 2v2: hidden until the fight

- **Your buildings stay secret until the fight.** Everything your team builds or changes this round is hidden from the other team until the fight starts, just like the squads you put down: a building your team votes for (or that the clock or a bot places), the Coal mine's entrance and exit, your Scorch turrets, a new Tree kojja, Magic lake water, and a Coal mine exit you dig somewhere new. Until the fight they see the board as it was when the round began (your old exit, with its 8 blocked tiles, still where it was). Your teammate sees everything at once. When the fight starts, everything shows. Buildings from earlier rounds are seen as before.
- **The vote is still public.** The other team knows which card you got (the artifact draft is open to all) and that you are placing it, but never where. No vote ever holds the deploy clock, so its timing doesn't tell them anything either. A card you get goes ahead of a kojja still to be grown.
- **Bastion walls too.** The other team doesn't see your walls of this round until the fight (as before), and now a wall of theirs you can't see never stops you putting one on that same edge. As the fight starts, the one put up later moves to the nearest edge it may stand on.
- **Nothing you try gives anything away.** Whether a tile is allowed is always judged by what your team can see, on your phone and on the server alike. So you may put a squad, water or a building right where the other team just put something hidden.
- **Same tile, new spot.** As the fight starts, with everything known: if two things ended up on the same tile, one of them moves to the nearest tile it is allowed on. **You always have prio in your own zone:** on a tile of your zone, yours stays and theirs moves, whichever was put there first. If theirs has nowhere at all to go, both stay; yours never moves for it. The edge tiles of your zone that are kept for their flank squads aren't yours for this: there, in no man's land, and between you and your teammate, the one put there later moves. That goes for squads, buildings, turrets, kojjas, water and walls alike. So an Oak your team votes onto a hidden Assassin in your zone stays, and the Assassin moves to the nearest tile an Assassin may stand on (not on or next to a building).
- **Nothing else moves.** Only things on the very same tile meet. An Assassin next to a building it couldn't see stays in that spot; a building closer than 2 tiles to the other team's hidden building or Coal mine exit stays; a squad on the tiles round an exit dug next to it stays. The Coal mine's exit never moves: one placed or dug where something of the other team's stands is just blocked (see the Coal mine). Water that with the other team's water shuts a tile off still moves (the newer tile), since the board must stay walkable.
- **No walls on the lake.** A Bastion wall never stands on water. You can't put a wall up on water you can see, nor water on a tile with a wall you can see (your own, your teammate's, or theirs from an earlier round). A hidden wall and hidden water on one tile settle like any two things as the fight starts: the wall goes to the nearest edge it may stand on, or the water to the nearest tile it may go on.
- A squad in a building stays with it: a Tree kojja's Nest squad, a School's pupil. Your buildings are always in your zone, so they never move for the other team's things. Nothing is called off and no gold comes back.

### 2v2: Undo all with team buildings

Buildings never vanish, so your teammate's buys stay where they made them.

A School enrolment of theirs that needed the seat you freed isn't such a move: their squad simply comes out again.

Water is never on a squad, building, the exit's 8 tiles, or other water, as the player sees the board; as the fight starts, a tile and a hidden squad or water of the other team's on one tile: the newer moves to the allowed tile nearest the one chosen (water on a hidden exit stays: the exit is blocked). Water put down this round is hidden from the opponents until the fight.
- "Never completely block in a tile or a walking path" read as: after the new water, every walkable tile of the board can still reach every other (4 ways, no diagonal squeezing between two water tiles).
- A lake tile can be moved only in the round it was put down; tiles not put down by the fight go down on their own (no man's land on the team's half first).
- Waterspirits: only the team that bought it walks on that tile; the 30% slow is the game's shallow-water slow; the regen is 5% of max health a second (the Oak's +25% healing counts).
- Water elemental: "level 2 Water Mage" = the second level. The elemental stands on the water at first; without Waterspirits on that tile it steps onto the bank at once. It counts as a unit for winning the round (as the Coin Golem) and holds artifacts as a summon (tier 1). Bought once per tile; it fights every fight.
- Undo all: refused while one of the teammate's building actions this round only works because of the presser's (a skill bought on the presser's new water tile, water put where the presser's exit stood). Before, the teammate silently lost the action and its gold.

**Jail and School**
- Jail is "technical" (his text) though the brief's table had it with the defensive ones; 1500 health, 8 armor.

The jailed team finds out at the fight (until then they may still move or sell it; a squad sold or put in their School before the fight refunds the 100). The jailed squad sits out the fight of the round it was jailed in.

- "Freed unit types can't be jailed again": every unit type that was locked up in the jail when it fell, for the rest of the match, whoever owns them. A squad jailed in the very round the jail falls was never locked up: it goes free with no ban, and the jailer doesn't get the 100 back (the squad did sit that fight out).

- "Can't be used next round": no jailing, no Skywatch and no guards, but the jail still stands in the fight (a target).
- Skywatch's 3-tile range is a square (the 7 x 7 tiles round it), matching the lit tiles.
- Jail guards: the Keep's 4 Wisps-based guards; they hold until an enemy is in their awareness (or one hits them), then fight as Keep guards do. They count as units for winning a round, as the Keep's do.
- School: either player may put in one of their own squads; one squad in all. Not a rider, a carrier with a rider, a squad moved this round, or a building. A squad put in this round can't be taken back (unplaced) while inside.

- While a squad learns skill k, its player can't buy the other skill of k's either-or pair for gold (they would end up with both). To switch, Stop learning (only the round it started) or wait.
- "A level of XP": the XP between the threshold its XP has reached and the next one (a squad not levelled up yet still gets the next step); none at the top level. Levelling up still costs gold. Double study gives two steps' worth.
- A skill learnt in the School is the unit type's (skills are per unit type in this game), so the player's other squads of that type get it too; it is free and counts like any bought skill for the price of the next one. One at a time; "Stop learning" only in the round it was started.
- The squad inside can't be sold (take it out first, free). Taking it out is hidden from the opponents until the fight (they still see it in the School), and so is putting it in (they see it on its tile).
- School destroyed: the squad that landed the killing blow gets half a level's XP (its own next step); the squad inside loses all XP and levels it gained inside, including levels bought with gold (no refund), and any skill being learnt; skills already learnt stay.
- Tapping the School opens its sheet (with the squad inside and a Sheet button for the squad's own sheet) rather than the squad's sheet straight away.
- Undo all by your teammate can take back the School seat your enrolment needed (their squad goes back in, yours comes out onto its tile): read as fine, since enrolling is free.

## 3 November 2026 – Balance study: what makes a unit strong

No gameplay changes in this one: it's the groundwork for the next balance passes. Over 10,000 simulated fights in the game's own engine, plus 200 Balance Lab matches, measured how much each stat is worth in gold. Full write-up in `docs/STAT-LEVERS.md`.

- **Health × damage is one stat.** A unit's raw power is the square root of health times damage per second, per gold. Trade them 1 : 1 in percent.
- **Range is the strongest stat:** each metre is worth about 5% gold. A melee unit needs about twice the health and damage of a 14 m unit to break even, and in Balance Lab matches long-range squads survive fights 35% of the time against 1% for melee.
- **Speed only helps units that have to walk.** Doubling it is worth +20% at 14 m and almost nothing for artillery.
- **Armor and ward are counters, not power:** armor beats many small hits, ward beats a few big ones.
- **Splash under 2.5 m does nothing**, because units always stand at least 2.45 m apart. From 3 m it's worth about +30%. Earth Wizard, Steel Wizard and Ent currently have 2 m splash.
- **Hidden costs:** slow projectiles (about −10%) and wind-up (−16% for 1 s).
- **A level-up is the best deal in the game:** about +50% gold's worth compared with buying more squads.
- **A baseline unit** for future units to be measured against: 100 gold, 3 bodies, 147 health, 19 hit at 1 attack a second, 14 m range, 3 m/s. It scores even against the roster, early and late.

## 2 November 2026 – Our own game server: tournaments and live matches

No Mans Land now has its own game server. Tournaments and live matches against friends run on it.

### The server is the referee
- **No more cheating in live matches.** Until now the host's phone ran the match and simply believed what the other phone said about its gold and army. Now the server keeps the match: your phone only says what you do (place, move, sell, level up, buy a skill, unlock, spin...), and the server checks every action against the rules before it counts. A changed game can't give itself gold, units it hasn't unlocked or squads on tiles it may not use.
- **Secrets stay secret.** The server only sends your phone what you're allowed to see: your opponent's new squads, upgrades, sales, moves and picks stay hidden until the fight, and so do their leader and packages in round 1. Before, the host's phone had everything.
- **Both players are equal.** Nobody has to be the host who keeps the page open. The server runs the deploy clock and the fight; it waits for a player who drops out (up to 2 minutes), and a reloaded phone goes straight back into the match (the link, or **Back to match** on the menu).
- **Play a friend** now gives a `play=` link. Your opponent opens it, or types the 5-letter code on the start screen.
- **Tournament matches** start by themselves once both players are in, and the result goes from the server straight to the tournament, so nobody can report a fake result. Spectators watch through the server too.

### Tournaments live on the server
- **Tournaments keep going without the organizer.** The tournament lives on the server, not on the organizer's phone. The organizer can close the page or run out of battery, and everything is still there when they come back. Players stay connected the whole time.
- **Safer:** every player (and the organizer) gets a private key when they join. Nobody can report a result for someone else's match or use the organizer's buttons.
- **Practice vs bots:** the server plays the bot-against-bot matches, so they finish even if your phone goes to sleep.
- Tournaments are removed from the server when the organizer ends them, or after two weeks without use. Old tournaments from before this update can't be resumed.

### Phone to phone still works
- The old phone-to-phone matches stay as a backup: **Factions** matches, your own **Counter Lab units**, old `join=` links, and the **Phone to phone instead** button in the lobby. If the server can't be reached at all, a new match goes phone to phone by itself. Organizers can switch a tournament's matches to phone to phone in its settings (**Matches run on**).
- vs AI, solo and the Counter Lab work without the server, as before.
- Next: accounts with "Sign in with Google" (and Apple), and later ranked play once there are enough players for short queues.

## 31 October 2026 – Tournaments

### Host a tournament
- New on the start screen: **Host a tournament**. Send the invite link (or the 4-letter code) to your friends. They open it, pick a **username**, and wait in the lobby. Works for any number of players; 6 to 12 is the sweet spot.
- Formats: **Swiss + playoffs** (everyone plays every round against someone on the same score, then the top 4 play semifinals and a final) or **Knockout**.
- Each round, your match shows on the tournament screen: tap **Start match** (if you host) or **Join match**. The match starts by itself when you are both in, and the result is sent by itself when it ends.
- **Standings**, **Matches** and a **Bracket** for the playoffs, live on every phone. The organizer can also share a **big-screen link** for a TV or laptop.
- Your opponent's username shows at the top of the screen during the match.

### Organizer tools
- **Settings:** format, Swiss rounds, playoff size, map (fixed, or a random one each round that everyone plays), look, Standard pack or All units, which leaders and units are in, **how many leaders to choose from (2 to 5)**, deploy timers (round 1 and later rounds) and the results screen timer.
- **Same rolls for both** (on by default): both players get the same leaders, starting units and tower choices to pick from.
- Set or change any result, replay a match, swap who hosts, rename or drop a player, undo a round's pairings.
- **Change who plays who:** in Matches (or Bracket → Change who plays who), tap **Change pairings**, tap a player, then the player they trade places with. Works in Swiss rounds and in the bracket, can move the bye to someone else, and in the playoffs can bring in a player from outside the cut. Only matches nobody has played yet can change; matches that change get new codes.
- If a game was already going on a match you move, both players get a **Match moved** screen and are sent to their new match; that game's result never counts.
- The tournament survives a reload of the organizer's page; everyone reconnects by themselves.

### Practice vs bots
- New on the start screen: **Practice vs bots** makes a tournament of you and **7 bots** (Bot Rusty, Bot Sprocket and friends), so you can try the whole thing alone.
- Bots play each other by themselves on the organizer's phone: real matches, played out by the AI with the tournament's rules, so the standings make sense.
- When you're drawn against a bot, tap **Play vs Bot …**: it's a normal match against the AI on your phone, with the tournament's map, timers and leader rules, and the result goes in by itself.
- In any tournament lobby the organizer can **Add a bot** (or **Remove bots**), for example to fill an odd number of players.

### Best of 3, 5 or 7
- New setting: **Knockout matches: best of 1, 3, 5 or 7** (in Swiss + playoffs it's for the playoff matches; Swiss rounds stay single games).
- Each game is its own match: after a game, your tournament screen shows **Game 2 · 1–0 · first to 2**, and you tap Start or Join again. A drawn game doesn't count.
- The organizer can add a game to either player, undo the last game, or restart the series.

### The bracket
- A real **bracket picture**: every round, winners moving on, TBD slots for what's still to come, series scores, the matches being played right now (gold) and the champion.
- It comes up by itself on your phone between playoff rounds, and there's a **Bracket** button on the tournament screen.
- Before the playoffs it shows the **projected** bracket: what the standings would give if the Swiss rounds ended now.
- The **big-screen link** now shows the bracket across the whole screen with the standings under it, made for a TV or laptop.

### Spectating
- Not playing right now? Open **Matches** and tap **Watch** next to a match. You see the board while they deploy (squads placed this round stay hidden until the fight, so no one can tip anyone off), every fight live, and who took each round.
- Works from the big screen too. When a game of a series ends, **Watch the next game** takes you along.

## 29 October 2026 (night) – Gold cap and shutdown bounty

### Gold carry cap
- You can carry at most **500 gold** into the next round. Anything above that is lost when the fight starts.
- If you press Fight or Ready with more than 500 gold, a warning tells you how much you'd lose, so you can keep shopping or go anyway.

### Shutdown bounty
- Win 3 rounds in a row and a 🔥 with your streak appears next to your health.
- The opponent who breaks the streak earns **+100 gold**, plus **+50** for each win past 3 (4 in a row pays 150, 5 pays 200).
- A draw ends both streaks without paying anything.
- The loser's +50 gold (from round 3) stays as it was.
