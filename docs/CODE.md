# Code: source files and tools in detail

Moved out of the README on 2026-11-11 so sessions only read the part they need; the text is unchanged.

## Editing

The source is in `src/`. After changing it, run `node src/build.js` from the repo root. This rebuilds `index.html`, `lab.html` and `lab3d.html`.

- `engine.js`: the battle engine. Plain arithmetic only, so both phones in a live match play the same fight. Draws nothing.
- `game.js`: match rules (gold, rounds, maps, characters, the AI, what each player may see of the other). Draws nothing.
- `shapes.js`: how a unit looks: its shape, colour and 3-letter tag.
- `art.js`: the shared palette, coats of arms and tower icons, used on the board and in the panels.
- `camera3d.js`: the 3D camera and its touch, mouse and key controls (strategic, tilted, angled and ground views, in that order: spreading two fingers or scrolling goes one step in, pinching or scrolling back one step out; tilted shows your side of the board up close at 50°, angled is lower and further back at 26°). The tilted view can be rotated round the middle of the board like the cinematic views (twist two fingers, right/middle drag, Q/E); the turn carries on into the views further in and goes back to none at the strategic view. It also zooms in on the final seconds of a high-stakes fight (the battle cam). In the strategic and tilted views (and the angled view while the player is placing) the page can take taps and one-finger drags for itself, to place and move squads.
- `render3d.js`: draws the board and the battles in 3D (three.js), through the same `view()` and calls as `render2d.js`, with the same markers (health, wind-up and flank timers, statuses, charisma, levels, buffs, tags, badges, placement hints, coats of arms, the elf spires, ice walls and hail, the crippled-army notice). The map floor is painted by `render2d.js` (its `floor()` call) and laid on the ground. Markers follow the camera: health as rings in the strategic view and bars in the angled view; the ground view is purely cinematic (no markers, no zones or grid on the floor).
- `models3d.js`: the low-poly models: units (soldier, archer, caster, goblin, brute, flier, horse, spider, drone), picked from each unit's stats, and their own models for the tier 4 units (Ether Golem, Sun Priest, Meteor Caller, War Mammoth, Necromancer, Truespear), Ent, Rogue, Sniper, Slimeshots, Skeleton Archers, the Obelisk (and its awakened eye) and the wizards (each with its element on the staff, the Frost Wizard an ice crystal), a tower for each tower type and one for faction towers (in the faction's colour), the elf spire and the thrown spear.
- `lab3d.js` + `lab3d-template.html`: the 3D test page: runs matches against the AI, with blue's deploy in the 3D view, and shows them with `render3d.js`.
- `render2d.js`: draws the board and the battles on the canvas. It reads what to show only through the `view()` the page hands it, so another renderer (3D) can take its place.
- `site-template.html` + `game.css`: the game page: panels, menus, live matches, music. The menu playlist is every song that isn't deployment or battle music (`PLAYLISTS`, `MUSIC_COMBOS`), played in random order: a random song first (a new player's first is Super coffee, `FIRST_SONG`: no sound settings saved and the menu music never played on the device, `pb-first-song`), then another random one each time a song ends, never the same twice in a row (a song picked in the music sheet plays on its own instead). Deployment and battle music follow the match's look, not its map (`LOOK_MUSIC`, `Game.lookOf`): Darkwoods and Autumn play the Mushroom Cavern combo, Mountain Pass (look id `snow`) and Moonlit Snow the Icy Peak combo, Ashlands the standard deployment music with its own battle song (Joe biden, `ash` combo), the rest the standard one. Songs in `UNASSIGNED` (Evening, S4knandet, the battle cut of Eyes in the front 2, and the battle songs Broski and DawnOnU, 2026-11-07, levelled to about -15 LUFS) are deployment / battle music not given to a look yet: they stay out of the menu playlist and All music and play nowhere until they go in `PLAYLISTS` or `MUSIC_COMBOS`, except in the **DEV** playlist (2026-11-10): a tab after All music ('dev') that only developer accounts get (`devMusic`: `devMode()`, or before the account code has run the account kept on the device). Developers can also put those songs in their own playlists; for everyone else they are left out of the + picker and out of any playlist of their own that has them (`songOk`), and signing out of a developer account takes the tab away at once (`acctSave` → `musicDevChanged`). It only hides the songs in the page: the MP3s are public files on the site. Check: `node tools/tests/music-dev-ui.js` (fake game server, own static server on 8767). A song can play just a part of its file (`start` / `end` in seconds on its `SONGS` entry): it starts at `start` and loops back there at `end` (`eyesInTheFront2Battle`, 22.15 to 88.61 s; the menu's `eyesInTheFront2` plays the whole file). New songs are levelled to the others' loudness (about -15 LUFS integrated, 192 kbps MP3). On the start screen the board behind the title shows the map and look picked in Match settings (`menuPre`), and a pick fades the title card back for a moment (`peekMap`). The start screen has a music player (`playerHtml`, `wirePlayer`; 2026-11-07: dark glass with low-poly facets, a teal glow, flat lists and tabs, gold trim and corner brackets, and glossy gold orbs for Play / Pause and the note beside Now playing; `--mp-*` colours and layers; the style is written up for reuse across the whole UI in `docs/UI-STYLE.md`): Now playing (the playlist playing and its song), a progress bar, then Shuffle, Back (to the start of the song, or in its first 3 seconds to the one before), Play / Pause (this visit only; Music Off in the Sound sheet is the lasting switch), Next and Repeat. Its icons are Kenney's Mobile Controls (CC0); Back and Next are made of its play triangle and pause bar. Music anywhere (2026-11-05): a note button (`#music-btn` beside the cog in a match, hidden in battle; `#music-fab` top right in the waiting room, draft and tournament screens; `#lob-music` on the Rooms list; Settings → Open playlists) opens the playlist panel (`openPlaylist`). Playlist panel (2026-11-07): Now playing on the menu rolls it up out of the menu's own player, which stays put (`mpui.mode` 'anchor', placed by `mpPlace`); anywhere else a player rises from the bottom of the screen with the panel on top ('dock'). Folder-style tabs (`mpTabs`): in a match first `<look> Adaptive music` ('map': the map's deploy and battle songs, named after the look on a map that wears one, `adaptiveName`), then Menu Playlist ('menu'), All music ('all', `ALL_SONGS`), the player's own playlists ('u:<id>', `myMusic.lists`) and + (make one from All music, songs in the order tapped; Edit, Delete with a second tap). A tab's Play or a song plays that playlist (`playFrom`, `pl.use`): on the menu it is `snd.src`; in a match it is "my music" (`music.mine`, `mine.on`, `mine.src`), which plays instead of the map's combo in deploy, battle, the result and match over until Adaptive music or the match changes (`matchKey`: mode, seed and code). The player's own playlists are kept on the device (`phonebellum.playlists.v1`) and, with the Sound settings, on the account: `musicChanged` sends them to /account/music a moment after a change (`musicPush`), and every answer carrying the player brings them back (`acctTake` → `musicFromAcct`: the account's copy wins unless this device has changes it never sent, which are merged in; another account's playlists stay with it). The player's buttons work the playlist that is playing (`ctlPl`). Check: `node tools/tests/music-ui.js` (own static server on 8762) and `node tools/tests/music-account-ui.js` (the account side, with a local game server; own static server on 8766). Players online (2026-11-10, developers only): every open page is counted by the server's OnlineRoom, and a developer sees how many are online now: a pink pill under their name on the phone menu, the first item in the Diorama's top bar with the DEV tag ([docs/SERVER.md](SERVER.md) "Players online"; checks: `server/test/online.test.js`, `node tools/tests/online-ui.js`). Autoplay (2026-11-10): the music tries to start as soon as the page has loaded (`autoMusic`); where the browser allows this site to autoplay (Chrome / Edge after the player has played sound here often, the game installed as an app, the site's Sound set to Allow, Firefox with autoplay allowed) it plays with no tap, elsewhere the first press, key or tap anywhere starts it (`unlockMusic` on pointerdown / mousedown / touchend / click / keydown). Browsers don't let a page get round that block. Keys typed in a text field are text only: every page key handler and the 3D camera ask `typingIn` first, and the camera lets go of held keys when a text field takes focus (`tools/tests/chat-pc-ui.js`, port 8763, also checks the PC talk button in the top left corner). Sound effects (`audio/sfx/`, `SFX` and `SFX_ACTS`) play through Web Audio: game actions (place, move, sell, level up, skills, Master skills and awakening, unlock, any undo) make their sound only when this screen's player did them by hand, so the AI and the opponent stay silent; every other button makes a plain click (menu click outside a match). Four UI sounds (2026-11-09): Ready (`ready`, only when the fight doesn't start at once: then the battle sound plays; the opponent's Ready plays it on this screen too, `foeReadySfx`, unless this player is ready already), Create room / Join / Quick play (`room`, `ROOM_BTNS`, and Enter in a join-code field), the tower and leader picks (`pick`, through `SFX_ACTS`), and a soft tick when the mouse moves onto a button outside a match (`hover`, mouse only, once per button; at 70% when it comes within half a second of the last one, `HOVER_RUN_MS` / `HOVER_RUN_VOL`). The Sound sheet has their own On/Off and volume. The artifact build inlines them (`window.PB_SFX`). Check: `node tools/tests/sfx-ui.js`. On a phone in a match the board fills the screen and the deploy panel is a drawer docked at the bottom, slid up or down on a blue pull tab (Settings → Phone layout: Below the board brings back the panel under the board); picking a unit puts it down and shows a line over the board with an ✕ to stop placing. On a PC the panel sits beside the board. Its board input works on tiles, so the 2D and the 3D board share it. The game starts on the 3D board; the in-match Settings (the cog) switch between it and the 2D board, saved on the device. `?board=3d` in the address forces 3D for testing, `?board=2d` starts on 2D. Only then does it load three.js (`THREE_URL`: `vendor/` here, jsDelivr in the artifact) and the 3D code from `src/`, and it falls back to the 2D board with one line when 3D can't load or start, the phone drops it, or fights run too slowly. The 3D board draws only while something moves; when forced for testing it shows a speed meter in its top right corner (tap it to change the quality).
- `template.html`: Counter Lab.

`vendor/` holds three.js (0.180.0, MIT licence), used by the 3D test page.

## Tools (`tools/`)

- Match history: server matches are saved by the game server itself (step 4, [docs/SERVER.md](SERVER.md)); the start screen's Match history sheet reads them from `GET /history` (`openHistorySheet`, `histLoad` in `src/site-template.html`). Test: `node tools/tests/server-history-ui.js` (needs the local page, peer and game servers). `tools/match-history.gs` saves phone-to-phone (PeerJS) matches to a Google Sheet, which is also the sheet's Phone-to-phone tab and its fallback when the server can't be reached. Paste it into the sheet's Apps Script, deploy it as a web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone) and put the `/exec` URL in `HISTORY_URL` in `src/site-template.html` (already set to the live sheet; a new deployment gets a new URL, so update it then). The hosting phone of a phone-to-phone match then saves every round. The URL is public, so anyone could add rows; delete junk rows in the sheet.
- `node tools/build-artifact.js` turns `index.html` into `phone-bellum.html`, the version published as the claude.ai artifact (PeerJS and three.js from jsDelivr, the 3D code inlined, no Counter Lab link). It also makes `lab3d-artifact.html` from `lab3d.html`, for the 3D test artifact, and `game3d-artifact.html`, the game forced onto the 3D board with the speed meter, for testing 3D on phones (publish `vendor/` next to both).
- `node tools/miro-sync.js` redraws the Miro factions board from `src/factions.json`, `src/default-roster.json` and `src/keywords.json` (run by `.github/workflows/miro-sync.yml` on push to main; needs the `MIRO_TOKEN` secret). See `docs/MIRO-SYNC.md`.
- `node tools/counter-table.js` recomputes `src/demo-units.json` (the counter table) after editing `src/default-roster.json`, and `src/faction-units.json` after editing `src/factions.json`. Rebuild afterwards.
- `node tools/tests/snapshots.js before` (before a change), then `node tools/tests/snapshots.js after before` (after it): draws the board at about 70 fixed moments, exactly the same every run, and lists any that changed. Use it when a change shouldn't alter what players see, like moving code between files. Only compare runs from the same machine. Needs the same servers as the other checks below.
- `tools/tests/game3d.js` plays the game on the 3D board (`?board=3d`): dragging, placing and taking back, the tower and unit sheets, the Move ability and placing in the angled view, and that the board stops drawing in the shop. Like `deploy3d.js` it needs Chromium's software WebGL (set in the script).
- More 3D checks, all with software WebGL: `game3d-maps.js` (the elf tower, the ice altar and Charisma on the 3D board), `game3d-mp.js` (a live round with both phones on 3D), `foe3d-mp.js` (a 2D phone against a 3D one: the 3D board never shows more of the opponent than the 2D board; rejoining), `board-switch.js` (the 2D / 3D setting and the fall back to 2D) and `artifact3d.js` (the artifact builds with the 3D board; run `node tools/build-artifact.js` first).
- `tools/tests/upgrade-ui.js` checks the range rings, Level all, Skip asking first and Look at board on both boards, and a guest's Level all in a live match. It sets up board states through `window.__pb()` and `window.__pbRedraw()` (both only with `?test` in the address).
- `tools/tests/` holds Playwright checks. The ones written for the 2D board open their pages with `board2d.js`, which starts them on 2D as if the player had chosen it. `PB_BOARD=3d node tools/tests/<check>.js` runs them on the 3D board instead (those that use `board2d.js`'s `tap`). They expect the game served on port 8766 (`python3 -m http.server 8766`) and, for two-player tests, `node tools/peer-server.js` on port 9000. Install with `cd tools && npm i`.
