# Gameplay: rules and the match screen

Moved out of the README on 2026-11-11 so sessions only read the part they need; the text is unchanged.

## Rules and features

- **Tutorial:** four guided rounds of one match that build the screen up step by step: your deployment zone blinking, your tower ("Protect it"), one card, a tile, Fight. The enemy's hidden Skeleton Archers win round 1 (how damage works); in round 2 their army is in sight and Fire Mage, which beats crowds, wins it. Round 3 opens up a little: the tower pick (Bastion or Keep only, a line on each; `m.towerOffer` fixes the offer) and its skill (a glowing panel button), a shop of five basic units with the deployment cap (the enemy adds a hidden Brute). Round 4: a level-up (its sheet also has Move, Sell and skills), the flanks and no man's land. Then the enemy plays for real, as the AI, with the full shop and the clock, and one coach line on that first screen says so. Tutorial fights play at 1x; the X on the coach line leaves. The menu leads with it until it's been played through round 4 (saved on the device), then it's a small button. The script is `TUT` and `panelTutorial()` in `src/site-template.html`; `tools/tests/tutorial-ui.js` checks it on both boards. Rounds 1 and 2 come out the same wherever the player places (checked headlessly over the zone's tiles), so after changing those four units' stats, check the script still holds.
- **Tutorial puzzles (2026-11-03):** the guided Tutorial below is switched off (no menu button leads to `startTutorial`; `tutorial-ui.js` can't reach it). The menu's one **Tutorial puzzles** tile (`#puzzles-open`, gold until a puzzle is solved, plus the newcomer button `#tutorial-new`) opens the list: three one-fight puzzles against an army you can see (its squads are placed as if from an earlier round), with a set budget and a small shop (`src/puzzles.json`, inlined by the build as `PUZZLES`; the server reads the same file). **Levels (2026-11-05):** each puzzle has a `level`; the sheet groups them (`.puz-level`) and the coach line says e.g. "Medium 2 of 3". Easy: The horde, Two giants, Out of reach. Medium, each with a new idea: Hold the line (a tough unit in front: Ent before Fire Mages, against Spiders), The long way round (squads march down their own side: goblins down the far side past a Brute), Through the wall (Earth Wizard stones go through the first target, with Snipers). Enemy squads are placed one by one as if from an earlier round, so a puzzle can have more than one round's deployments (Through the wall has four). Played in the tutorial's mode (`tut.puzzle`, `startPuzzle`, `puzResult`): Old standard board, 1x fights, no clock, the coach line over no man's land (`.coach.mid`) with a Hint button; a loss offers the hint and Try again, a win is saved (`phonebellum.puzzles.v1`) and offers the next one. **Initiate title (2026-11-03):** solving the three Easy puzzles gives the light blue Initiate title (Medium ones don't count). The server believes no claim: each win is sent as the army that won (`POST /account/puzzle { k, army }`, `puzResult` -> `puzzleSync`), the server sets the fight up as `startPuzzle` does and replays it with the referee (`accounts.js puzzleWon`); wins made signed out wait on the phone (`phonebellum.puzzle-wins.v1`) and go with the next account answer. Checks: `server/test/invites.test.js` (puzzle section), `node tools/tests/initiate-ui.js`. `tools/tests/puzzles.js` checks headlessly that each answer wins from most placements (a Medium answer where its hint says to put it) and the tempting wrong answers lose (run it after changing those units); `puzzles-ui.js` plays one in the browser.
- **Player health:** each player starts with 100. The loser of a round loses health for every enemy squad still standing (by tier: 3 / 6 / 8 / 12, `TIER_DMG`), but never more than 35 a round (`MAX_ROUND_DMG` in `src/game.js`). A player whose tower falls in a fight also loses 10 health (`TOWER_FALL_HP`), but the round's damage and the tower's together are still at most 35: a round that already costs 30 costs 5 more for the tower, one that costs 35 nothing more (`towerHp` in the round's summary).
- **Economy (2026-10-28, against waiting for gold and then massing tier 4):** the loser's +50 gold only from round 3 (`LOSER_BONUS_ROUND`). Everyone may take a loan once a round on every map from round 1: +100 now, 200 less next round (`LOAN`); the Bank tower's Credit line makes it +200 / 300 (`CREDIT_LINE`, `loanTerms`), and a bank still does one thing a round. Major investment: pay 500, get 800 next round, once per match. The bot only borrows with a Credit line. `tools/tests/economy-1028.js` checks all of it.
- **Carry cap and shutdown (2026-10-29):** at most 500 unspent gold goes into the next round (`CARRY_CAP`); the rest is lost before the new gold comes in (`summary.capLost`). Pressing Fight or Ready with more asks first (`capCheck` in the page). Breaking an opponent's streak of 3+ round wins pays 100, +50 per win past 3 (`SHUTDOWN`, `shutdownGold`, `m.streak`, `summary.shutdown`); a draw ends both streaks and pays nothing. A live bounty shows as 🔥N by that player's health. The loser's +50 stays. `tools/tests/economy-1029.js` and `carry-cap-ui.js` check it.
- **Towers (2026-10-28):** Keep: Muster (trait: 1 extra deployment every round, automatically, `musterOn`; since 2026-10-29 nothing to buy, and one bought before picking the Keep is refunded), Brute guards 400. Sniper tower: Drop paratroopers 400 (since 2026-11-05; it replaced Deploy airship and its +30 hit for Snipers and the tower): at fight time 3 s, 3 paratroopers (the roster's Wisps as they are, 1 body each, instant shots, squad -4) land in a small triangle about a tile in front of the enemy tower, each hitting every enemy within 3 m for its own hit (`PARA`, `paraDropOf`; the tower body carries `paraDrop`, the engine's timed `drops`, landing spots shown on both boards until then via `bt.drops()`); Mark Bounty (`MARK`, 50 once): one enemy squad from an earlier round is marked for the match (`m.mark[side] = { squad, round }`, hidden from them until that fight), Snipers deal it +15% and see it through stealth (engine `sniperMark` / `marked`), and a fight where a Sniper takes its last body pays 100 (`res.markKill`); it can't be moved, and if the squad is sold it can be placed again for free. Bastion: Fortify (`FORTIFY`, 50 a round: one wall for this round's fight only, a `temp` wall in `m.bwalls`) and Vault (trait: treasury +6 armor, raids take half, `VAULT`). The Overwatch skill also sees stealthed units within its range (`u.trueSight`). The bot marks the priciest enemy squad and sometimes fortifies. `tools/tests/towers-1028.js`.
- **Towers (2026-11-03):** Bastion's March order (trait `freeMove`): one free move every round. Free moves (`freeMoves`, `freeMovesLeft`: March order plus Otto's horseshoe move) are used before the paid one, by just moving the squad (`sq.luckMove` = the round it used one; moving it back gives it back); the sheet says Move · free. Since 2026-11-07 March's free move reaches at most `Game.MARCH.tiles` (2, Chebyshev) from the squad's tile at the round's start (`sq.from`); `Game.moveKind(m, side, sq, col, row)` → `'free'` / `'paid'` / null is shared by `moveSquad` and the move highlights (render2d `moveKinds`, render3d `collectDeploy`: refused tiles hidden, paid-only tiles dim), and every drop re-checks it, so a paid move dragged back within reach is refunded. Nothing new is stored on the squad. The P2P host counts a guest's `luckMove` as March only within `Game.marchNear` (advisory). The Mage tower has Muster too (+1 deployment every round, like the Keep). `tools/tests/towers-1103.js`.
- **2026-11-03:** Eagle eye (`TOWER_SKILL.eagleRange` 1.5, `eagleAps` 5) works for every unit of the side (not buildings); Snipers and the tower get `eagleSniper` (2) times that (`eagleBy`). Mark Bounty's `sniperMark` goes on every unit's bodies, so every unit gets +15%, sees the mark through stealth and can earn the bounty. Spiders: Poisonous fangs, Spiderlings or Cavestalkers, then Web-shots (type `web`, `u.web`: `webStep` shoots each body's webs at the nearest pickable enemy in range, `webOn` stacks `v.web` { slow, dmg, t } and lands the shooter's poison and slow; the slow joins the strongest-slow rule, `hurt` cuts a webbed attacker's hit by `web.dmg` %). A skill's own `text` shows on its sheet; the full `describeSkill` text goes in the hold-to-read part.
- **Encyclopedia:** a start-screen button opening Units, Towers, Leaders and Maps (`openEncyclopedia()` in `src/site-template.html`).
- **Health in fights:** every unit shows a health ring (strategic view, 2D) or bar once it's hurt; small units (under 500 health) get a thinner one.
- **Range on hover:** with the mouse over a unit, on either board, while deploying or in a fight, a dotted circle in its side's colour shows how far it reaches. Tapping a squad shows the same ring (and opens its sheet), a squad just placed shows it at once, and in a fight the body tapped shows it too. Both rings are drawn in an SVG over the board (`boardMarks()` in `src/site-template.html`, which also draws the tutorial's blinking tiles) from one measure: `Game.squadReach` for a squad (its skills, Eagle eye, the map's cut and Test Grid's +2 m, a flame cone's 1.2× reach; an enemy's as far as this screen may know it: the skills and awakening you have seen, a disguise as what it pretends to be), `Game.towerReach` for a tower, and for a fight's body its live reach as the engine has it (Flow state stacks, Sightweavers' cut). Before, hover and tap measured differently (the tap ring left out flame cones, the hover ring Test Grid's +2 m and Eagle eye). Since 2026-11-07 a lane enemy is found on any tile of its block (`ringAt`, `blockOf` at `seenAt`), 3D hover goes through `pick()` (camera3d passes clientX/Y to `onHover`), and in 2v2 `hoverRange` / `tapRange` also cover the teammate (`Game.mateAt`) and the other opponent (`Game.teamSeen`): `squadRing(side, sq, team)` measures on `duoSheetMatch(side)` with that lane's skills, in that player's colour.
- **Level all:** a squad's sheet offers Level all when the gold covers two or more level-ups of that unit's squads: since 2026-11-07 it works in passes, one level at a time: each planned squad counts the levels the plan gave it (`n`), and the pick is the ready squad with the lowest `n`, then the lowest level, then the most XP, then the oldest id, so every ready squad gets its next level before any squad gets a second one, until the gold runs out. The AI's `aiLevelRun` is unchanged. `Game.levelAllPlan` / `levelAll`; it is one `levelUp` per squad, so a guest's Level all reaches the host the same way single level-ups do.
- **Skip to result** asks first: the first tap turns it into "Sure? Tap again to skip" for 3 s, like selling.
- **Match over:** Look at board hides the end screen so the final board can be looked at (hovered, and turned on the 3D board); Show result in the panel brings it back.
- **Intent:** tapping one of your squads while deploying draws an arrow from it to the enemy squad it means to go for first, with a dashed ring round that squad, but only when that squad already stands within its awareness where the fight begins (its range + sight, at least the minimum sight: what the engine spots at once). A pick further off depends on walking there first while both sides move and the opponent's new squads (unseen) can get in the way, so it shows as a dashed arrow the way the squad walks, with no unit named. Two arrows at most, with a small count of bodies on each when it splits. Its sheet says it in words. A unit picked in the shop previews the tile under the mouse or finger (dragging a card), and a squad dragged to another tile previews that tile. It is worked out by `Game.intentPreview` (`src/game.js`): a short fight played by the real engine from what this screen may see (`Game.seenBy`: the opponent's squads from earlier rounds where `seenAt` has them, nothing they did this round), until each body has picked an enemy to walk to, then grouped by enemy squad; each board state is worked out once and kept. It shows intent, not who wins, how much damage is done or how the fight ends. In a fight a tap on a unit (either side) shows its card and where its squad's bodies are walking right now, with small rings on whom they attack: the engine keeps whom a body walks to (`b.intent`) apart from whom it attacks (`b.lock`), which can differ. `node tools/tests/intent.js` checks that the preview picks exactly what the real fight picks when nothing is hidden; `tools/tests/intent-ui.js` checks the page on both boards.
- **Direction arrows (2026-11-04):** a melee squad (base range up to `AIM.melee`, 5 m; never a rider or a building) may get an arrow from its sheet (Set direction, then a tap or a drag out of the squad), at most `AIM.maxTiles` (5) tiles, free, in every gamemode. `sq.aim = { dc, dr }` is the end in tiles from the squad's tile, so it moves with the squad and stays until cleared (`Game.setAim` / `clearAim` / `aimTo` / `aimEnd`, also referee actions). In the fight (`bodiesFor`) each body gets the engine's `b.guide` (the devtest path) to its own spot shifted by the arrow, plus `b.guideT`: sight and awareness are off until it gets there. Since 2026-11-07 it only stands to fight an enemy in its reach that is ahead of it (within 60° of its way, engine `GUIDE_AHEAD`; one beside or behind is hit in passing), and it gives up after `AIM.stall` (3) s without getting `GUIDE_GAIN` (0.5 m) closer (`b.guideT` is that budget, `b.guideBest` / `b.guideIdle` track it; after a push-back `b.guideBest` follows the body out to `gd - GUIDE_GAIN`, so walking back counts but jostling on the spot doesn't; time fighting, stunned or rooted doesn't count). **Head drag (2026-11-07):** `boardPress` → `aimHead(t)` grabs the head of one of your arrows (`Game.aimEnd` on that tile) when no squad of yours stands there (`aimDrag.head`); dropped on its own squad it clears. **Flank cap (2026-11-07, no player text):** a squad on a flank tile of its side (`Game.isFlank`) reaches at most `AIM.flank` (1; 2v2 `AIM.duoFlank` 2) tiles, as a square; `setAim` keeps the stored arrow and `Game.aimCap` / `aimUse` cut it where it is used (`aimTo`, `aimEnd`, `bodiesFor`), so page, P2P host, referee and replays agree (an old replay with a long flank arrow now plays back cut); render2d `aimFloor` draws a (2n+1)-tile square for it. Hidden from the opponent while deploying (`Referee.hide`, `seenBy`, the PeerJS `syncState`). Drawn on the floor (`aimFloor` in `src/render2d.js`, so the 3D board gets it). Checks: `node tools/tests/aim.js`, `node tools/tests/aim-ui.js`, `node tools/tests/fixes-1107-combat.js`, `node tools/tests/fixes-1107-C.js` (`fixes-1107-C-ui.js`: head drag, flank, March, hover rings, 2D and 3D).
- **Awareness** (`docs/AWARENESS.md`, `AWARENESS` in `src/game.js`) is part of the core fight, on in every gamemode: an engaged body picks whom to walk to within its sight by distance, its lane and crowding (melee slots), keeps its current one unless another is clearly better, and a new attack target skips an enemy its side is already about to kill. Counter Lab's sandbox fights stay as they were.
- **The tray (UI 2.0):** on a phone the deploy panel is a tray under the board that never covers it: gold, deployment pips with a +1 chip, the clock and Fight / Ready in one row, a hint line, then the shop grouped by tier (a small tier tab with the price, the units you have unlocked, and a + Unlock space while that tier has units left; it opens that tier's locked units). Drag a card up onto your zone to place it (a tap on the card, then a tile, also works; a hold opens its sheet; with a mouse the wheel or a sideways drag scrolls the tray). Drag a squad from this round down onto the tray to take it back, or an older one to sell it (asks first); dragging an older squad to another tile is the Move (50 gold, once a round). Swipe the tray down (or tap the handle on its top edge) to fold it to its top row and see more of the board; swipe up or tap SHOP to open it again (it opens by itself at the start of each round). The tutorial keeps its ✕ marks. Plan: `docs/UI2-PLAN.md`; standalone prototype: `ui2-test.html`.
- **Play:** open the site and tap Challenge a friend. Send the link, and your friend opens it in any browser. No accounts needed.
- **Counter Lab** (`lab.html`): a balance sandbox for designing units. Units saved there show up in the game under Load units on the same device.
- **3D test** (`lab3d.html`): real battles on the 3D board, with low-poly units, to try the 3D renderer and camera. You deploy blue against the AI in the strategic view (or set Blue: AI to watch): pick a unit in the shop and tap a tile, tap a squad from this round to take it back, drag it to move it, Auto lets the AI deploy for you. Blue has unlimited gold here. You can also place and drag in the angled view while deploying; there a tap places instead of going back, so use the Strategic / Angled / Ground buttons (or pinch out, scroll, Esc). Strategic view straight down; spread two fingers (or scroll) for the angled and ground-level views, drag to look around, twist (or right/middle drag, Q/E) to rotate, tap (or Esc) to go back. Fight, Next round and New armies drive the match.


## Otto - The Gambler (slot machine)

Leader `otto` (All units only). `Game.SLOT` holds the numbers: 50 gold a spin, 3 a round, odds skull/shoe/coin 12%, goblin 3%, chest 1%. Outcomes come from `slotOutcome(m, side, round, k)` (seeded by the match, side, round and spin number), so both phones agree; `slotReels` gives the three symbols shown. `spin()` applies a spin here (gold, health floored at 1, the goblin squad: `gift: true`, level 1, unlocked, not counted as a deployment, can't be taken back). Horseshoes (since 2026-11-03) give, at random, +1 deployment or +1 free move every round for the rest of the match (`shoeKind`, `shoePerks`, `shoePerk`; the spin's record keeps `shoe`: 'deploy', 'move' or 'gold'); one of each at most, so the next horseshoe gives the other, and with both it pays `SLOT.shoeGold` (200). The host's `takeSpins` works out the same perks. Multiplayer: the guest sends `slots` (its spin count) and the host replays them with `takeSpins` for the health cost; the host's round 1 spins are hidden like its leader. A spin resets Undo all. Bot and Claude opponent: `aiSlots`. UI: `openSlots` / `doSpin` / `foeSlotNews` in `src/site-template.html`. Tests: `tools/tests/otto.js` (headless), `tools/tests/otto-ui.js` (browser).
