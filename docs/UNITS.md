Only a developer still sees the Units choice (Standard / Custom). A device that had Learn the game saved plays Standard. - No mage or slime tower: the round 3 offer is drawn from the Keep, Sniper tower, Bastion and Bank tower.

## Shiv, Water Mage and bleed

- **Unit traits:** a unit's `trait: { name, text }` shows on its sheet as "Trait: name". Shiv: Apply bleed; Water Mage: Waterhealing; Steel Wizard: Steel armor (6 armor).
- **Either-or skills:** `skillOr: [[1, 2]]` on a unit means a player can own skill 2 or skill 3 of it, not both (`Game.skillOr`, `canBuySkill`); the sheet boxes them together with an OR between them. The Shiv, Water Mage, Steel Wizard and Earth Wizard have it.
- **Upgrade rows** (`upRow()` in `src/site-template.html`): every upgrade (unit skills, tower skills, mage generals, the loan, the Mecha Tower's bots, building skills, the altar) is one compact row: its name and button, then one line of what it does, cut short. Press and hold a row (or Enter on it) for the whole text and details (what it needs, who it's for, the price rule). Choices that rule each other out are boxed with an OR between them (`orGroup()`): either-or skills, the mage generals, a building's skill of the round.
- **Tap a unit in a fight** (`inspectCard()`): a card at the top left of the board shows that body's health now, level, hit, attacks a second, range, speed, armor or ward, how many of its squad are left, the upgrades its squad fights with (bought skills, Enzo's free one, an awakening; built-in ones left out), and what's on it (stunned, poisoned, bleeding, burning, slowed, summoning). It updates live and goes when the body falls, a tap elsewhere or its ✕. Stealthed enemies can't be picked.
- **Bleed** (`BLEED` in `src/engine.js`): a unit with `bleedEvery: 2` gives a stack of bleed with every second hit it lands on the same target. A bleeding body loses 3 health twice a second for 2.5 s (each new stack starts the 2.5 s again; 48 a tick at 5 stacks); every stack doubles that, up to 5 stacks; at 4 stacks it also loses 2% of its max health a second, and at 5 stacks 5% (`BLEED.pct`). Bleed goes past armor. The stacks are the target's own, so every source adds to them. Too deep (`bleedDeep`): 1 s longer, and no healing of any kind while it lasts. Anemia (`bleedStun`): a stun of 1.2 s when the stacks reach 5. Titanium spears give a stack too. Drawn as a red drop with a pip per stack (2D) or a red dot per stack (3D).
- **Water Mage:** `waterHeal`: allies in its splash heal for its hit, and with no enemy in reach it shoots the most wounded ally in reach. Pool shaper (`pools`): its attacks leave pools for the rest of the fight that never overlap; every second enemies in one take 25% of the hit, allies heal it and get +0.5 m/s for 2 s. Flow state (`flow`): +0.1 attacks a second and +0.4 m range per attack made standing still, up to 5; walking takes them away. Master skill (2026-11-03): **Summon water elemental** (type `elemental`, `u.elemental`): at the start of a fight each Water Mage body gets a Water Elemental `ahead` m (9 = 1.5 tiles) in front of it (`createBattle`, squad −1, credit to her): 600 health, the attack of a level 2 Ember Imp, her move speed, levelled with her level; when it dies it leaves a pool (`u.deathPool`, radius 4, 50% of its hit a second). Pools: a side's pools can't overlap each other, but both sides' can, and a body takes one pool of each side at a time (mirror-safe since 2026-11-03).
- **Steel Wizard:** Heavy metal (+4 armor, +25% health), then Reforge (`aura`: +2 armor; allies within 8 m get +20 on every hit and +0.1 attacks a second) or Shape Titanium spears (`spears`: spends 1 armor per spear, 1.5 s to forge, 200 damage at 35 m/s and a stack of bleed).
- **Earth Wizard:** +25% health and 2% regeneration, then Stone spikes (+5 m range, two shots in quick succession, 20% slower) or Living armor (`slam`: +8 armor, −13 m range, a cone slam for 200 damage that stuns for 1 s; its cone is 25° each side, narrower than the Flame Wizard's 30°, set by the skill's own `cone`).
- Poisoned units heal 50% less (`POISON_HEAL`; slime units still drink poison in full) and move 20% slower while the poison lasts (`POISON_SLOW`; the stronger of this and any other slow counts; not slime units or Berserkers). `node tools/tests/poison-slow.js` checks the slow.
- `node tools/tests/shiv-water.js` checks all of this headlessly.

## The Assassin, Awaken skills and the unit rework

- **Master and Awaken skills:** units have 4 skill slots (`Engine.SKILL_SLOTS`). The 4th is a Master skill or an Awaken skill: `skillNeedsAny: [null, null, null, [1, 2]]` on the unit means it needs skill 2 or skill 3 first (`Game.skillNeedsAny`; skill 2 or 3 can't be taken back while it leans on it), and `flat: true` on the skill keeps it at its own price (300) without making the unit's other skills dearer (`Game.isFlat`).
  - A **Master skill** (`master: true` on the skill, `Game.isMaster`) is bought once and works for every squad of the unit, like any skill: the Water Mage's Summon water elemental, the Wisps' Spirit walk, the Brute's Berserkers.
  - An **Awaken skill** (any other flat skill, `Game.isAwaken`) is bought for one squad at a time, on that squad's sheet (`Game.awaken`, `undoAwaken`, `canAwaken`, `awakenBlock`): the squad keeps `sq.awaken` (the round it was bought) and only it gets the skill (`Game.unitFor` with the squad). It is hidden from the opponent until the fight (`Game.seenAwake`; the guest's sync drops it, the host's sheet of a guest squad too). A squad taken back refunds its awakening, a sold one counts it in its worth. A player can awaken only one unit type a match (`Game.wokeUnit`): any number of squads of the type they awakened first, none of another (`awakenBlock` says `'type'`); it holds from the fight on (`m.woke`, set in `resolve`), even once those squads are sold, and taking back this round's awakening frees the choice again. The host drops a guest's awakenings of another type. The Ember Imps' Summon hellfiend and the Leaf Wizard's Leafblade (the Ent's Explode on death is a Master skill now). An awakened squad has a gold ring round its bodies (2D) or on its tile (3D). The AI awakens squads now and then; Claude (Play Claude) gets `"awaken": [squad ids]`.
  - An **Evolve skill** (`evolve: true` on a flat skill, `Game.isEvolve`, `evolveSlot`) is never bought: a squad gets it by itself, free, after the fight in which it meets the skill's need (`needs: 'glutMax'`: its Glut is full; `Game.evolveReady`). `resolve` sets `sq.evolved` (that round) and lists it in the summary's `evolved`; it fights with the skill from the next round on, for the rest of the match. It works for Enzo too, isn't the match's awakened unit type, and both players see it (the gold ring and the Eye's model, `Game.shownAwake`). The Obelisk's Eye of the Obelisk.
  - The sheet labels them Master, Awaken or Evolve and says what they need.
- **Skills with more than one effect:** a skill can carry `also: [{ type, params }, ...]`, applied and described after its own effect (Longbones, Graverot arrows, Dread rangers, Forward planting, Bullhead, Sightweavers, Shadowborn, Overwatch).
- **Assassin** (tier 4, 500 gold, 1 body, 350 health, 500 hit, 0.35 attacks a second, 6 m/s, melee): stealthed (`stealth` on the unit), dashes the last 10 m to its target at 30 m/s without being seen (`dash`), deals 50% to buildings (`bldgPct`), and every hit it lands gives the target 3 stacks of bleed (2 until 2026-11-06; Bloodrush, `bleedHit`; with Death Flurry every blow does; `BLEED.max` still caps the stacks). Silent blade (its first attack doesn't give it away), then Death Flurry (3 blows of 200, 0.2 attacks a second; a kill leaps it to the nearest enemy within 10 m for the next blow) or Shadow hunter (hunts bodies with no ally within 3 m, `LONE_R`; none for 5 s and it fights as usual until its next lone kill). Both skills give `noTower` (ignore the enemy tower), but since 2026-11-06 the Assassin ignores **every building** on its own (`noBldg: true` on the unit, `ignores` / `othersThan(side, true)` in `src/engine.js`): it never picks, leaps to or hunts a structure (the tower, the treasury, watchtowers, walls, the Factory and other buildings, 2v2 MPBs: anything with `u.structure`) while any enemy that isn't one can be picked. With nothing else left to pick (say only a Sniper safe inside that tower, or stealthed units) it attacks the buildings after all, so a fight never stalls on it; and an enemy wall standing between it and the unit it walks to, within its reach, it may hit (`validTargets`), so a Bastion wall can't freeze it in place. Such bodies take the full scan on the fast paths (`!b.u.noTower && !b.u.noBldg`).
- **Assassin placement** (since 2026-11-06, `anywhere: true` on the unit, `Game.anywhere`): in every mode and map, 1v1 and 2v2, an Assassin squad may be placed or moved on **any tile of the board** from round 1: its own zone, no man's land, the enemy's zone and either side's flank tiles, as long as the tile is free as its player sees it and is not on or next to (the 8 tiles around) a building (`Game.nearBldg`, `bldgSeen`): either side's tower and treasury, the elf towers (the map's, between its middle rows, and the players' own), buildings (the Cog Builder's Factory, the Altar of Build), devtest lesser buildings, the 2v2 teammate's and the other pair's building squads, and 2v2 MPBs through `Game.mpbAt(m, col, row)` (a stub returning false until `src/mpb.js` replaces it; it is called through the api so it can). The opponent's buildings count only once that player may see them (from the fight of the round they were put down), so the rule never gives a hidden one away. An Assassin placed this round in the enemy's zone is hidden from them until the fight like any new squad (`Referee.hide`, `foeSeen`); they may put a squad on that same tile; as the fight starts (2v2: `Duo.settleBoard` in `Duo.runFight`) one of the two moves to the nearest tile it may stand on (Vilhelm, 6 Nov: "if things are placed on the same tile, they find another spot close to it"): on a tile of a team's own zone the other team's ("You always have 'prio' in your own zone", so an Assassin in the enemy's zone is the one that moves), in no man's land the one put there later; and from the next round on it holds the tile. In its first fight one in the enemy's zone or on a flank tile stands still `FLANK.delay` (3 s) as flank squads do (`bodiesFor`). The AI now and then drops one on the enemy's two back rows (`aiSpot`). The PeerJS host keeps a guest's squad from an earlier round on the tile it already stood on (its tile may since have become one next to a building the host is seen to have). `node tools/tests/assassin-anywhere.js` checks it all.

Forward planting (+10% health; squads may be placed in no man's land), then Reinforced Bark (+20% health, 4 armor) or Grasping roots (`roots`: each body drags one enemy 5–13 m away toward itself at 1.5 m/s, 10 damage a second; it can still attack but not walk; once it is in reach the Ent fights it and grabs another). - **Sniper:** Ranger scope (+3 m), then Deadeye (+5 m, +20%, −35% attack speed, and each hit also deals 5% of the target's max health past armor: `pctHit`) or Assaultgunner (1 attack a second, −8 m, −50%). Awaken skill (since 2026-11-03): **Hunter** (type `hunter`, `also` stealth): an awakened squad marks one enemy squad from an earlier round on its sheet (`Game.huntMark`, `undoHunt`, `canHunt`, free; `sq.prey = { squad, round, was?, wasRound? }`, moved any round). ## Frost Wizard and the Obelisk

Two tier 3 units (300 gold, unlock 100), in All units (not in the Standard pack). Balance notes, the simulations behind the numbers and what to playtest: [docs/FROST-OBELISK.md](FROST-OBELISK.md).

- **Frost Wizard** (900 health, a 25 m beam of 21 damage, 3.2 a second: even with Glacial gaze one hit can't kill a goblin of its level, 26 health): its beam puts **Frost** on the target (`frost` on the unit, `u.frost` in `src/engine.js`): a stack on the first hit and one more every 2.5 s the frost keeps coming, up to 3 stacks: 20% / 30% / 40% slower moves and attacks. The stacks melt 2 s after the frost stops. They are the target's own, so two Frost Wizards on one target don't stack it faster. Units that can't be slowed (Berserkers) keep their speed. Skills: Deepfreeze (a 4th stack freezes the enemy solid for 2 s, not towers, and the stacks start over), then Multibeam (a second beam on a different enemy; each beam keeps to its enemy, `beamLock`) or Glacial gaze (+5 m, +20%, it beams the enemy in reach with the most health, units before towers: `healthiest`). Master skill: **Snow storm** (needs Multibeam or Glacial gaze): every enemy within 8 m of it takes its hit and its Frost as often as it attacks (`storm`).
- **Obelisk**, a giant serpent (1300 health, 90 hit, 0.7 attacks a second, 4 m/s, melee): it heals for 50% of the damage it deals (Devour, `lifesteal`), and it eats the enemy bodies it kills, and those that fall within a tile (6 m, `Engine.EAT_R`) of it, whoever killed them; not buildings or constructs. Each is a stack of **Glut** (`glut` on the unit): +6% health and hit, and it grows on both boards; up to 3 a round and 15 in all. The stacks stay on the squad all match (`sq.glut`, `Game.glutOf`; the fight reports them in `res.squads[..].glut`, `resolve` keeps them, `unitFor` puts them in its stats and `bodiesFor` starts its bodies on them). A violet ring on the squad fills up with its Glut. Skills: Gluttony (one more a round), then Hunger's haste (+0.1 m/s and +1.5% attack speed per stack) or Crushing maw (a 3 m splash). Evolve: **Eye of the Obelisk**, free and by itself once a squad's Glut is full (`evolve: true`, `needs: 'glutMax'` on the skill), Enzo's Obelisks too: its attack becomes ranged, 18 m (`ranged`), with its own 3D model (a hood and glowing eyes).
- Drawn on both boards: the snowflake and obelisk shapes (2D), the Frost Wizard and the coiled serpent with its head reared (3D), frost beams, Frost pips, a block of ice while frozen solid, the snow storm round the wizard, Glut rings and a violet wisp when the Obelisk eats.
- `node tools/tests/frost-obelisk.js` checks all of this headlessly.

## Towers: Keep and Bastion

- **Keep, Guards:** the 4 guards stand one on each side of the keep (front, behind, left, right) for 5 s. A guard that is hit leaves its post at once and goes for whoever hit it (`b.guard` and `b.avenge` in `src/engine.js`).
- **Bastion:** its attack is a moat of fire (`MOAT` in `src/game.js`: 12 damage 4 times a second to every enemy within 5 m); it has no shot of its own. **Home ground** also gives it a shield that blocks its first 2 hits each fight. **Defensive walls** (free since 2026-11-06, was 250 gold; still bought once a match; `noFree`, so Blixten's discount isn't spent on it; the AI takes it at once; replacing Moats) gives the bastion +4 armor and 3 walls (`BWALL`), each along one edge of a tile of your own half of the board (not the board's outer edge), from the tower's sheet: tap near a tile's edge and the wall goes along it (`m.bwalls[side] = [{ col, row, edge }]`, `Game.EDGES`; an edge is the same from either of its tiles, `bwallOn`; an older wall without an edge stands on its tile's front edge). A wall takes no tile: squads stand on both sides of it. Walls have 400 health and 4 armor, stay all match and stand again in every fight; one placed this round can be tapped to take it back, and walls not placed by the fight go up by themselves in a line along the front edges two rows in front of the tower. The opponent sees new walls only from the fight on. In fights a wall is a long thin barrier (`u.barrier` and `u.barrierY`, its half length and half thickness, in `src/engine.js`): enemies can't walk into it (they walk up and hit it) and their shots at anything behind it hit the wall instead (lobbed shells fly over); its own side walks and shoots through. `tools/tests/bastion.js` checks all of this.
