Keyword icons, wave 1

Drawn from src/keyword-registry.json. A keyword is a glyph; its role is the plate behind it (shape and colour). Shown at 76, 34 and 22 px on the plate, then the bare glyph at 22 and 14 px for the smallest uses. Colours are placeholders.

The six roles: a plate each

A plate with no glyph is the role alone. A unit that is simply a Guard (a tank) shows the plain shield.
