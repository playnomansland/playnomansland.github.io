Keyword icons, wave 1

First drawings from src/keyword-registry.json. Each at 72, 28 and 16 px in its role colour, and in ink on saffron. Role colours are placeholders.

StrikeDamage

Hits one enemy up close.

ShootDamage

Hits one enemy from range.

SplashDamage

Hits everything in a small circle.

PierceDamage

Hits every enemy in a line.

ShredDamage

Cuts a share of the target's health.

TankGuard

Takes a lot of damage.

RushGuard

Charges in and knocks enemies back.

PullControl

Drags an enemy to it.

BurnAfflict

Fire damage over a short time.

SlowAfflict

Enemies move and attack slower.

PoisonAfflict

Damage over time, past armor.

BleedAfflict

Stacking wounds that hurt over time.

HealSupport

Gives health back.

CarrySupport

Carries a squad into the fight.

HideSupport

Unseen until it attacks.

RevealSupport

Sees hidden enemies.

SpawnSummon

Brings more bodies into the fight.
