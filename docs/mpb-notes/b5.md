# Group B5: the Shadow Den and the Factory (2v2 buildings)

## Patch notes

- **The Shadow Den** (technical building, 800 health, 2 armor). While your team has it:
  - Rogues, Assassins and Shadowhands placed on a flank tile (an Assassin also in the enemy's zone) don't stand still the 3 s flank delay in their first fight, and (since 6 Nov) don't wait the 1.5 s coming up a Coal mine.
  - **Shadowhands** are in your shop (tier 1, 100 gold, no unlock): a Rogue at half health and half damage, 1 body, stealthed. They can go in no man's land.
  - **Shadow deployment:** each of you gets 1 more deployment a round that only a Rogue, an Assassin or a Shadowhands can use.
  - **Impersonate** (200 gold, once a round each): pick an enemy tier 1 or 2 squad you can see. 5 s into the fight one of its bodies becomes a Shadowhand of yours.
  - **Sabotage** (100 gold, once a round for the team): pick a unit type. Next round the other team can't deploy it. They see a big red X on its card.
  - **Sabotage** keeps working through its round even if you buy (or take back) a new Sabotage that round.
  - **Insider information** (350 gold, once a round for the team): next round your team sees the other team's board live as they change it (squads, and since 6 Nov this round's buildings, water, exit, turrets, kojjas, walls and vote tiles; not their gold, buys or levels), and their deploy clock is 15 s shorter (never below 5 s left after a pause). They see "You are being watched". If your Den is destroyed in the fight of the round you bought it, they get it for free next round.
  - **If the Den is destroyed:** you lose all your Shadowhands squads (no gold back), and no Den skills the next round. It stands again in the next fight.
- **The Factory** (economic building): the Cog Builder's Factory as a team building. It is built at once, no Cog Builder needed. Each round either of you can buy one of its units for the team: Cogbot Fighter (100), Cogbot Machinegunner (100) or CogMech (300). Whoever buys pays, and the unit shows in their colour. One unit a round per Factory. A unit bought fights in that round's fight only: it doesn't come back next round (its sheet's Read more says so, since 7 Nov). If it is destroyed in a fight, it is being rebuilt and makes nothing the next round, and the board shows it out of action (greyed, with a small crossed-cog badge, since 7 Nov). A Cog Builder's own Factory still works beside it. It flies a big team banner, so you can tell it from a Cog Builder's.

## To check with Vilhelm

- Shadowhands are a unit of the 2v2 set that only a team with a Den sees in its shop (recruiting = placing it like any unit, 100 gold, tier 1, no unlock). They keep the Rogue's speed, range, attack speed and Stealth, get Forward planting built in (no man's land), and the Rogue's third skill can be bought for them. They level and sell like any squad (sell: half of 100).
- "Shadow deployment" is read as one extra deployment per player per round, usable only by Rogues, Assassins and Shadowhands; your normal deployments never grow from it.
- "0 deployment time in flanks" covers the Assassin's delay in the enemy's zone too, and (Vilhelm, 6 Nov: "Den removes the wait for rogues, assassins etc.") the wait after coming up a Coal mine.
- Impersonate's timer: 5 s. The Shadowhand it makes fights only in that fight (it isn't a squad afterwards); the body taken out doesn't count as a kill. Only squads the buyer can see are targets (an enemy squad placed this round is hidden, unless you are watching them with Insider information).
- Sabotage locks the type for both opponents for the next round (placing new squads only; their squads already on the board still fight and move). Any unit type may be picked.
- Insider information shows the other team's squads live (where they place and move them), and since 6 Nov their whole board (buildings, water, exit, walls, vote tiles), to both watchers, but not their gold, buys, levels or Vault. Their clock (75 s of a 90 s round) runs out 15 s early: after that they can't act but the fight starts at the normal time. After a pause the cut never leaves them under 5 s.
- Sabotage bought two rounds in a row: both work, each in the round after its buy.
- Impersonate on a squad that is sold (or taken back) before the fight: nothing happens and the gold is spent (the buyer can take it back during the round). A Shadowhand made by a lane-1 buyer has that player's Shadowhands skills. "Destroyed while active" is read as: destroyed in the fight of the round it was bought.
- Den destroyed: the Shadowhands squads are removed (no gold back); Shadowhands made by Impersonate during that fight keep fighting to its end. "No Den skills next round" blocks Impersonate, Sabotage and Insider information; recruiting Shadowhands and the Shadow deployment keep working.
- The Factory MPB is not levelled, gets no builder skills (Master engineering etc., even if the buyer has a Cog Builder with them) and no Blessing, and has no build site: it simply stands. Its downside reading ("as the Cog Builder's"): destroyed in a fight, it makes nothing the next round (the Cog Builder's must be built again).
- The Factory's units fight for the team; their XP and damage count for the Factory, not for the buyer's squads.
