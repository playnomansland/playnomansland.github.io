# Group B3: Coal mine and Magic lake (2v2 buildings)

## Patch notes

- **Coal mine** (technical): your team votes for the entrance in your zone, then for the exit in no man's land. Squads deployed on the 8 tiles round the entrance go down the mine when the fight starts and come up 2 seconds later on the matching tile round the exit, then stand still for 1.5 seconds (as a squad new on a flank tile) before they fight (not a Shadow Den's Rogues, Assassins and Shadowhands, since 6 Nov). While they are underground nobody can see or hit them. Nobody (either team) can deploy on the 8 tiles round the exit, even where they are the other team's zone. If the tile a squad would come up on is taken as the fight starts (a squad or any other unit, building, water or wall of either team; since 6 Nov, was: an enemy squad, water or a building), that squad stays where it was; if the exit's own tile is taken, nobody goes down. The entrance can be destroyed (squads already on their way still come up); the exit can't be hit. Since 7 Nov a mine destroyed in a fight is shut the next round: nobody goes down it that round (it still stands in the fight as a target, its exit still blocks the 8 tiles, and both skills can still be bought); destroyed again that round, it is shut again the round after.
  - **Dig a new exit** (150 gold, then 50 more for each earlier dig of this mine; once a round, since 7 Nov: was once a match): move the exit, anywhere in no man's land or to a flank tile of the enemy's half. Buy it and the glowing tiles show at once: tap one. Each buy is one move, used the round you buy it (one not used is lost when the next round begins). You can take it back the same round only while the old exit tile is still free.
  - **Fresh air** (200 gold, once a match): squads coming up the mine (and the healbots and Water Elementals that come with them) are 25% faster and have +4 armor for 5 seconds.
- **Undo all** (2v2): refused while your teammate's moves this round rely on yours (for example a skill they bought on your new water tile), so nobody loses gold or a tile to someone else's take-back.
- **Magic lake** (technical, terrain): each of you puts down 2 water tiles of your own anywhere on your zone or in no man's land, flank tiles too (press Put down water, then tap a glowing blue tile), never on squads or buildings. Water is shot over but never walked on, and nobody can deploy on it. Water can never shut off a tile or a way across the board: a tile that would is not offered. A tile you put down can be moved until the fight; one you didn't put down goes on its own before the fight, and so does one that turns out to be on a squad the opponents hid there this round. Water can't be destroyed.
  - **Waterspirits allowal** (50 gold, per tile): your units can walk through that tile, 30% slower, and regain 5% health a second while on it.
  - **Become Water elemental** (200 gold, per tile): every fight a Water Elemental (as a level 2 Water Mage's Summon water elemental) rises from that tile and fights for your team. The tile stays water.
