Before you post

  • Limits. 2,000 characters a message and up to 10 files. Without Nitro each file must be 10 MB or less; everything here is well under that.
  • Links. A bare link gets a preview card. Wrap it as <https://…> to keep the message clean when you've attached pictures.
  • Order. Discord shows files in the order you attach them. Four pictures make a neat 2 × 2 grid; three leave a gap.
  • Headings. # headings and -# small print only render in messages, not in embeds or channel topics.
  • Spoilers. In a server that discusses balance, consider ||spoiler bars|| around strategy tips.
  • The preview card comes from the showcase page's link tags, so it appears once the showcase is live on the site.
