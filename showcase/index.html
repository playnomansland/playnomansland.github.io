Strategy you play before the fight.

No Mans Land is a strategy auto-battler for two players. Each round you get gold, buy squads from a shop of four tiers, and place them on your half of the field.

Your opponent does the same, out of sight. You can see the army they had last round, but not what they add this round. When the clock runs out, the fight plays itself: Fire Mages lob fireballs into crowds, Snipers pick off big targets from across the field, goblins swarm the front line, and a War Mammoth charges through the middle of it.

Then the board stays. Your surviving squads carry into the next round, you see what beat you, and you adapt. Counter their giants with a horde, out-range their horde with mages, level up the squad that carried you, or take a loan and gamble. Each squad left standing costs the losing side health. Last commander standing wins.

How a round works

Three phases, with the same names the game shows at the top of the screen. The match goes on, round after round, until one commander runs out of health.

1Round · Deploy

Spend and place. Buy squads, unlock stronger tiers and drop them on your side. Move an old squad, level one up, or point a melee squad down a lane. Ninety seconds on the clock.

2Round · Battle

Hands off. Everything you couldn't see is revealed and the armies collide. Watch it from straight above, or pinch in and follow the fight at ground level.

3Round · Result

Pay up, then adapt. The loser takes damage for every enemy squad still standing. Gold comes in, survivors stay put, and the next round starts from what you just learned.

Outthink them. You can't outclick them.

Blind bets

Your opponent's new squads stay hidden until the battle starts. You plan against the army you saw last round, and against the one you think is coming.

Counters everywhere

Fire Mages melt swarms. Snipers punish giants. Earth Wizard stones go straight through the front line. There is always an answer, if you can afford it.

Towers and leaders

Pick a Keep, a Bastion, a Sniper tower or a Bank, and draft a leader with a rule of their own, from Friggo's tougher troops to Otto the Gambler's slot machine.

Money is a weapon

Take a loan now and pay it back later, make one big investment per match, or break an opponent's winning streak to collect the bounty on it.

Learn with puzzles

Short one-fight puzzles teach the counters against an army you can see, with a budget and a small shop. Solve the easy three to earn your first title.

Play anywhere

It runs in the browser on PC and phone. Play the computer, send a friend a link, join a public room, or climb the ranked ladder.

Eight battlefields

Each look brings its own scenery and light, and several bring their own deploy and battle music. Same rules, different mood.
